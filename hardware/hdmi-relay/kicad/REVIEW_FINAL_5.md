## Verdict

**Safe to pay for Y7.** No Blocker was found. Y7's Gerbers, drill files and job file are the current board (0 differing
lines against a fresh export of HEAD; stackup JLC04161H-3313 in the job file), the BOM and CPL match the board
(175 placeable designators each, J301 correctly held out for hand fitting, sides correct, no DNP part included), every
CM3 pin and every RK628D ball lands on the intended function, the I/O-bank supplies on the V1.322 module put every
carrier signal on a 3.3 V bank, the power tree and the RK628D reference circuit check out against the datasheets, the
overlay compiles, applies and binds the right drivers, and the case fit check passes with plug probes.

The two Majors below (PCIe TX routing, U104 thermal headroom) are accepted design limits of rev A that affect
margins, not correctness, and both have a documented mitigation (Gen1 fallback; the 2 A sustained cap and the
thermocouple test). The one item that cannot be closed offline is JLCPCB's model orientation for the parts the
Oct 27 viewer check did not name (m12); those rest on the Oct 25 check recorded in `gen/fab.py`, whose CPL rows are
unchanged in Y7, and none of them is destructive if wrong.

Counts: Blocker 0, Major 2, Minor 15, Note 14.
