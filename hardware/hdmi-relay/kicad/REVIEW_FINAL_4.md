## Verdict

**Safe to pay for Y7, on one condition: confirm JLCPCB's placement-viewer orientations on the Y7 line itself (M1).**
Y7's Gerbers, drill files and job file are byte-for-byte the content of a fresh export of HEAD (only `CreationDate`
differs); the stackup in the job file is JLC04161H-3313; the BOM is consistent with every footprint and every pinout I
could check at pad level; the CPL equals the board for all 175 rows apart from the 25 deliberate JLCPCB corrections.
Nothing in the schematic or layout is a defect that would justify a re-spin before five prototypes: the electrical
design checks out against the datasheets, including the I/O-bank question (PMUIO2 really is 3.3 V on the V1.322
module) and the RK628D reference circuit.

What remains is (a) the orientation of JLCPCB's part models, which only their viewer can show and which the repo
records as checked on Y6 (all polarised parts) and on Y7 (J101 only); a 180° error on U201, U102–U106 or C102
destroys the board, so look at the Y7 line once more before paying; (b) two device-tree defects (M2, M3) that would
leave HDMI-in and Bluetooth dead at bring-up but need no copper change; (c) the printed case's front wall, which
stops an HDMI plug 1.6 mm short of the socket (M4) and must be fixed in `case.py` before printing; (d) the two
accepted layout risks carried from earlier reviews, now quantified more exactly (M5, M6).

**Counts: 0 Blocker, 6 Major, 20 Minor, 12 Note.**
