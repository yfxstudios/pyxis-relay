## Before paying

- **CM3 revision.** Our carrier follows module revision **V1.32 or later**. RS's listing doesn't say which revision
  it ships; ask RS or check the board marking on arrival. An older module (V1.3 / V1.31) puts five signals on the
  wrong pins (REV_A_SPEC.md section 1).
- **RS account:** the RS site showed "Your company account is blocked and you cannot place orders" while adding to
  the cart. Sort that out with RS, or check out as a guest.
- **The CM3 is the Wi-Fi version (W2).** Fine for development (DESIGN.md); the production part is the no-Wi-Fi
  RM116-D2E16W0, which nobody had in stock today (Allnet: out of stock; Keyestudio: sold out).
- **DC plug:** the board's jack has a 2.0 mm centre pin; a 2.1 mm plug fits but can be loose.
- **Mouser** asked for a bot-verification slider, so its parts aren't in a cart: upload the CSV in Mouser's cart
  page ("Upload a spreadsheet").

## Not in the carts (check you have them)

- M2 × 3 screw and an M2 standoff for the Wi-Fi card at H501 (match the M.2 socket's height, TE 2199230-4).
- A 9 V USB-C PD charger (any 20 W+ PD charger) for the USB-C power test.
- A micro-SD card for the IO board (the CM3 has eMMC, but the IO board can boot Radxa OS from SD).
- An HDMI source that outputs 1080p60, and an HDMI monitor.
- Case hardware for printing a test case (case/README.md).
