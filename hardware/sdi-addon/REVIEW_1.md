# SDI add-on rev A: design review 1

Date: 2026-10-27. Reviewed at commit 9d62461 (branch `hardware/hdmi-relay-design`). Independent review of the
schematic (`kicad/gen/*.py` and the generated `kicad/*.kicad_sch`), the two project footprints, `DESIGN.md`'s
placement and case plan, and the relay-side interface. No project file was changed; this report is the only write.

## Verdict

**Not ready for layout yet, but close.** The schematic is electrically sound: all 100 balls match the datasheet,
the input network, AGC, VBG/LF, VCO filter, crystal, straps, reset, GSPI, decoupling and the SY8089 buck all check
against the GS2971A Rev. 3 and SY8089 datasheets, the J602 pin map matches the relay pin for pin, every J602 signal
is on a 3.3 V CM3 bank, and ERC is clean (0 violations with every severity enabled). Nothing in the schematic has to
change before layout starts; the few schematic items below are value/strap choices that can be made during layout.

What blocks layout is the physical plan, not the circuit:

1. **The planned stackup cannot make a 75 Ω trace** (B1). On JLC04161H-3313 a 75 Ω microstrip over L2 is about
   0.07 mm wide, under JLCPCB's 0.09 mm minimum and a fifth of a 0402 pad. The stackup (7628 prepreg, or a plane
   cut-out under the SDI path) has to be decided before the first trace.
2. **The BNC position in DESIGN.md does not fit** (B2). With the centre pin at x 113.4 the rear leg holes land on
   the J301 socket body underneath, and the front shoulder already sits 1.85 mm into the 2.2 mm wall. The connector
   needs about 1 mm more, and the wall boss has to be designed around the new number.
3. **The case plan has no assembly path** (B3). A bulkhead BNC through a vertical wall cannot be lowered into place
   with the lid, and nothing holds the add-on except the 2.00 mm socket. The mounting scheme (notches vs. holes with
   spacers, nut or no nut) decides the add-on's outline, so it has to be settled before the outline is drawn.

Resolve B1–B3 (half a day of decisions, no schematic edits), then layout can start. The Majors are software,
interface-margin and sourcing items to carry into the layout and the bring-up plan.

Counts: 3 Blocker, 7 Major, 13 Minor, 6 Note.

## Verified as correct

Checked against the datasheets and the relay's actual files (not against DESIGN.md):

- **Ball map:** all 100 balls in `parts.py` agree with the Rev. 3 pin assignment and table 1-1; the generator's own
  `_check()` guarantees completeness, and the netlist confirms every stacked power ball is on its net.
- **Pin functions and straps:** 20bit/10bit HIGH (20-bit), IOPROC_EN HIGH, TIM_861 LOW (H:V:F), SW_EN LOW,
  STANDBY LOW, SDO_EN LOW (no loop-out; SDO/SDO-bar then pull high internally and may float), RC_BYP LOW
  (irrelevant without SDO), JTAG/HOST LOW (GSPI mode), CS pulled up, RESET pulled down, RSV B3/F2 unconnected,
  SMPTE_BYPASS/DVB_ASI as outputs in the default auto mode. All control inputs are Schmitt inputs without internal
  pulls (figure 3-1), so the 10k straps are needed and sufficient.
- **Input network:** BNC → 6.2 nH ∥ 75 Ω → node with 75 Ω to ground and 1 µF into SDI (C1); SDI-bar (D1) through
  1 µF to 37.4 Ω to ground. Identical to figure 5-1 (rendered and checked part by part).
- **AGC, VBG, LF, LB_CONT:** 470 nF AGCP–AGCN plus 470 nF AGCN–GND; 1 µF on VBG; 47 nF on LF; LB_CONT floating with
  DNP options to GND (double bandwidth) and 3.3V_A (half), per 4.5.1 and figure 5-1.
- **VCO supply:** 1.2V_A → 105 Ω 1 % → VCO_VDD (A4) with 33 µF to ground, as table 1-1 requires.
- **Crystal:** 27 MHz on XTAL1 (K6) / XTAL2 (J6) with ground-pad 3225 footprint; ESR ≤ 50 Ω and ±100 ppm noted;
  cap values to be matched to the crystal's CL (m10).
- **Supplies and decoupling:** CORE_VDD and PLL_VDD at 1.2 V (±5 % met by the SY8089's reference accuracy and 1 %
  divider), A/EQ/BUFF_VDD at 3.3 V analog, IO_VDD at 3.3 V; 4/3/3/4 × 10 nF at the ball groups exactly as figure
  5-1; 0 Ω (or ferrite) links to the analog rails with 1 µF/10 µF; the "3.3 V must track an external cable driver"
  note does not apply (no cable driver).
- **Power-up and reset:** any supply order is allowed (4.23); reset ≥ 1 ms after supplies (4.24) is in the CM3's
  hands with a pull-down default; all outputs are high-impedance in reset, so the DVP bus is never driven before the
  CM3 configures it.
- **GSPI:** four wires at 3.3 V to 3.3 V GPIOs; command word 16 bits MSB first, data on rising SCLK, SDOUT on
  falling (SPI mode 0); CS pulled up.
- **Output drive:** 8 mA at 3.3 V (VOH ≥ 2.4 V, VOL ≤ 0.4 V) into the CM3's 3.3 V inputs; LOCK LED at about 1.2 mA
  on STAT3 in parallel with the interrupt line is within the 8 mA.
- **J602 interface:** the 40-pin list in `sheets.py` is identical to `board_io_sheets.py`; 5V_SYS 1–2, 3V3_IO 3–4,
  GND 5, 6, 24, 39, 40; DVP_D0–15 on 7–22; PCLK 23; HSYNC 25; VSYNC 26; I2C 27–28; LOCKED 31 → GPIO4_A7; GSPI
  33–36 → GPIO4_A6, GPIO4_B0, GPIO3_A1, GPIO3_B4; RESET 37 → GPIO3_C1.

- **Relay geometry:** J602 at board (104, 31)–(106, 69), lid posts H602/H604 at (116, 36)/(116, 68) with Ø6.8 posts

## Software implications (for the relay's device tree and drivers)

- **DVP capture:** enable `rkcif_dvp` (and its `sditf` node) with pinctrl `cif_dvp_clk` + `cif_dvp_bus16`, bus width
  16, BT.1120 with embedded sync as the designed mode (M4), PCLK sampling edge and HREF/VSYNC polarity from the
  receiver's defaults, Y/C swap bit as found at bring-up (n2), and the 1080p50/60 delay-line sweep (M1, register 06Ch).
- **Pins taken back from the Radxa base tree** are already handled in `rk3566-relay-carrier.dtso` (GPIO3_C6/C7 and
  GPIO4_A4/B7 freed from the IO-board's regulator enables and LEDs); add `GPIO4_A6/A7/B0`, `GPIO3_A1/B4/C1` and the
  I2C4_M0 pins to the same list, and confirm nothing else claims `GPIO4_C1` (CIF_CLKIN).
- **Receiver control:** a V4L2 subdevice driver for the GS2971A does not exist in mainline or the Rockchip BSP;
  write one, or start with `spi-gpio` + `spidev` (mode 0, 16-bit, MSB first, ≥ 37.1 ns between words) and a user-space
  tool for: reset release timing (M6), standard detect (VD_STD register), Level B→A conversion and its 05Eh reset
  sequence (M2), STAT mux and H_CONFIG (M4), output drive strength 06Dh (M3), PCLK delay 06Ch (M1), audio enable.
- **LOCKED:** GPIO4_A7 as an interrupt (gpio-keys or the subdev's IRQ) to start/stop streaming; the LED mirrors it.
- **Reset:** `reset-gpios = <&gpio3 RK_PC1 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>` in the subdev node (or a hog), held low ≥ 20 ms after
  power, then ≥ 1 ms more; drive it low again when the stream is torn down if power is cut to the add-on.
- **ID EEPROM:** `&i2c4 { pinctrl i2c4m0_xfer; eeprom@50 { compatible = "atmel,24c02"; } }`; the relay software
  reads it to choose SDI as a source. If m6 (hardware SPI3) is taken, drop the EEPROM and identify the board by
  reading a GS2971A register instead.
- **UART9** (pins 29/30) stays unused by the add-on; leave it disabled in the overlay.
